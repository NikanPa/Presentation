# E-Commerce Product Operations & Business Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes a synthetic e-commerce dataset (5,000 records, 2023–2025) to extract actionable intelligence for product operations and overarching business strategy. The analysis moves beyond raw sales volume to evaluate profit efficiency, sales channel health, and high-value customer behavior.

## Key Business Questions Answered
*   **Executive Health:** What are the top-line revenue, total profit, and overarching profit margins of the business?
*   **Product Profitability:** Which product categories drive raw volume versus pure bottom-line efficiency?
*   **Channel Strategy:** How does the owned Website perform compared to the Mobile App and third-party Marketplaces? 
*   **Customer Activation:** Who are the absolute most valuable customers based on purchasing behavior?

## Methodology
The analysis is structured into four strategic phases:
1.  **Macro View:** Calculating vital signs (Total Net Sales, Margin, Average Delivery Days) for executive dashboarding.
2.  **Category Efficiency:** Utilizing dual-axis visualization to contrast raw Total Profit against Profit Margin percentages, revealing that while Fashion drives volume, Beauty and Home & Kitchen drive efficiency.
3.  **Sales Channel Optimization:** Evaluating margin performance across channels to recommend organic traffic shifts toward the highly profitable owned Website.
4.  **RFM Customer Segmentation:** Building a Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) algorithm to score customers into quartiles, successfully isolating the top 75 "444" VIP users for targeted retention campaigns.

## Tech Stack
*   **Language:** Python
*   **Data Manipulation:** `pandas`
*   **Data Visualization:** `matplotlib`, `seaborn`
1. Ensure you have Python installed along with the required libraries (`pip install pandas matplotlib seaborn`).
2. Download the `ecommerce_business_analytics.csv` dataset and place it in the root directory.
3. Run the analysis script to generate the console outputs and profitability charts.
